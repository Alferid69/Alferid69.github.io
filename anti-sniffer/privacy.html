Anti Sniffer privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026

Anti Sniffer ("the app") is made by Alferid Hassen ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and who else is involved. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we never sell data.

Information the app sends to us

To show the video list, the app contacts our server. Like any server, it receives your device's IP address and the time and address of each request. We log these briefly to keep the service secure and to stop abuse. The logs are kept by our hosting provider, Render, only for as long as its log retention allows, and are not used for anything else or combined with other data.

Each request also carries a security signature and, on versions installed from Google Play, a Google Play Integrity result. That result tells our server whether the app is the genuine copy from Google Play, whether the device passes Google's integrity checks, and whether the Play account obtained the app from Google Play. It does not include your name, email, contacts or files. Our server checks it and logs only a short pass/fail summary.

Checks that stay on your device

To protect the videos, the app checks the device for conditions that allow traffic to be intercepted or the app to be modified, such as:

  • an active VPN or proxy;
  • developer options or USB debugging being turned on;
  • root access, an emulator, a debugger or app-hooking tools;
  • whether specific known packet-capture, proxy, root or hooking apps are installed.

These results are used only on the device to decide whether the app can open. They are not sent to us or anyone else. The app does not read the full list of apps on your device; it only asks Android about the specific tools it looks for.

Third-party services

  • YouTube. Videos are played with the embedded YouTube player, and thumbnails come from YouTube. YouTube (Google) may collect information when you watch, under its own terms. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of that information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Integrity API. Google processes information about the app, the device and the Play account to produce the integrity result described above. See Google's Play Integrity data safety notes and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Render hosts our server and keeps its request logs. See Render's privacy policy.

Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted with HTTPS, and the app only accepts certificates from the authorities our server uses, so intercepting proxies are rejected. Screenshots and screen recording are blocked inside the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

Since we keep no account or profile about you, there is nothing to delete beyond short-lived server logs. You can stop all data handling by uninstalling the app. For questions or requests about your information, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
